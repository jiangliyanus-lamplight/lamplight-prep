Alignment with UNSDGs

All projects are aligned with United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. Each project selects 1-2 main SDGs and is evaluated by SDG indicators. Student portfolios are built in SDG language.

  • SDG3 Good Health: Young Doctors / Medical Club, The Bloody Raven
  • SDG4 Quality Education: Special Education Club, CAD for All / BEAM, Agora / Coding, TED-Ed Club / Outwrite, Family Forum
  • SDG5 Gender Equality: The Bloody Raven
  • SDG8/9 Decent Work & Innovation: Young Entrepreneurs, Business of the Future, CAD, Agora
  • SDG10 Reduced Inequalities: Special Education Club
  • SDG12/13/14/15 Responsible Consumption, Climate, Life Below Water & Land: RUP, PPR, Climate Justice Global Alliance, Reimagined Fashion Upcycling Club, MGL
  • SDG17 Partnerships: Climate Justice, MGL, Family Forum
